title: Speaking characters, voices and the finished film
version: 1
desc: Use when people speak in the film, to keep one voice per character, and to put the finished cut together.
words: \b(dialogue|dialog|speak\w*|says?|talk\w*|voice|voices|lip.?sync\w*|monologue|conversation|narrat\w*|final cut|finished (film|cut)|assemble|put (it|them|the shots) together|full film)\b
---
1. Every speaking character has one voice for the whole film. Look in the asset book for a voice entry under the
   character's name (a "voice" asset). When there is one, put it in "uses" in the voice slot of every shot that person
   speaks in. When there is none, say in "reply" that a short voice sample kept under the character's name keeps the
   voice the same in every shot, and offer to make one; plan the shots anyway.
2. The words go in the prompt exactly as the user wrote them, in quotation marks after "Audio:", with the name of who
   says them ("Audio: Maya says: \"We are late.\""). Give the shot the time the words need (about 2.5 words a second,
   plus a second before and after). One speaker per shot unless the user asks for a conversation in one frame.
3. Show the speaker's face when they speak: a medium close-up or a close-up, facing the camera or at three quarters, so
   the mouth is seen. Cut to the listener for the reaction in the next shot.
4. When the story is a whole film and the user wants it finished, end the plan with one "assemble" step: it puts the
   shots in order, with the music (an audio step before it, named by "after", or a sound the user has, named by "audio"),
   a title card and an end card. Nothing else follows it. Leave it out when only some shots were asked for.
5. The finished cut uses the take the user keeps of each shot; say so in "reply".
