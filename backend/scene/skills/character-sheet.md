1. A reference picture is an image, made with an image workflow, not a video.
2. Make it easy to reuse: the whole person (or chest-up when the user asks for a face), facing the camera or at three
   quarters, a plain light grey background, soft even light, nothing in front of them, no text.
3. Describe the person exactly and concretely: age, gender, skin tone, hair (colour, length, style), face, build, clothes
   and their colours, shoes, one or two details that make them recognisable. Use the user's words for everything they gave.
4. Keep the style the user asks for (photograph, 3D, anime, illustration); a photograph when nothing says.
5. Portrait shape for a full body, square for a face.
6. Fill "keep" with the type and the character's own name ({"as": "character", "name": "Maya"}): once made, the picture
   is kept in shared memory under that name, and every later shot and every agent tab can use it. One step per
   character, place or prop; a location is kept "as": "location". In "reply", say they will be kept under those names.
