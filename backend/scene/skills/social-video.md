title: A Reel, a Short, a TikTok or a creator-style video
version: 1
desc: Use for a Reel, Short, TikTok or creator-style video with a strong hook.
words: \b(reels?|shorts|tiktok|instagram|insta|youtube shorts|ugc|vlog|creator|influencer|story|stories|whatsapp|viral|hook|trend\w*)\b
---
1. Portrait, always, unless the user names another shape.
2. Hook first: the first shot is short (4 to 5 seconds) and starts on movement or a face close to the camera.
3. Three to five quick shots of 4 to 6 seconds beat a long one. Change the framing between shots (close, medium, wide)
   so the cut feels quick.
4. A creator or presenter the asset book has is used in every shot they are in. Someone speaking to camera: medium
   close-up, eye level, natural light, a phone-camera feel, the exact words in quotation marks after "Audio:".
5. Words on screen (a caption, a title, a call to action) are a "motion" step or an "overlay", never words in a video prompt.
