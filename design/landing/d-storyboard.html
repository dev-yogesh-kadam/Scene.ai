01 / How it worksThree moves

Board. Shoot. Cut.

1

Describe the shot

Write what happens and add reference pictures. Pin a cast to the project, and the same face, outfit and place carry from shot to shot.

2

Make takes on the canvas

Every result lands on a board, one row per shot. Start the next clip from the last frame of one you like, or roll it again.

3

Cut it together

Put clips in order on the timeline, trim them, add music, and export one video.
