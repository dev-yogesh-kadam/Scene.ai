Every take is kept. You circle one.

Each shot gets as many takes as you like, on a board with one row per shot. Start the next clip on the last frame of the one you circled, or roll it again. Nothing is thrown away until you say so.

I.

Describe the shot

Write what happens and add reference pictures. Pin a cast to the project, and the same face, outfit and place carry from shot to shot.

II.

Make the takes

Every result lands on the canvas. Keep the take you like, start the next clip from its last frame, or roll it again with a new seed.

III.

Cut it together

Put the keepers in order on the timeline, trim them, lay music under them, and export one video.
