Four directions for the landing page

Each is a different idea, not a recolour: its own layout, type, structure and moving parts. All four say the same true things about the studio and use the same drawn stills, so they can be compared on design alone. Open one to scroll it and click around.

A · The Monitor Open →

The first screen is a camera monitor: frame lines, a running timecode, lens readouts, a REC light, a plan typing itself on a slate. Switching the scene cuts the whole set.

Dark · technical · cinematic · black, white and one red

B · The Premiere Open →

A film poster and a magazine: ivory paper, a large serif title, a credits block, a contact sheet where the kept take is circled in red pencil, a playbill of the models.

Light · editorial · premium · ink and pencil red

C · The Screenplay Open →

The page is a script. The opening scene types itself while the shot develops beside it; every section is a scene heading, the agent speaks in dialogue, the models are the cast list.

Dark desk, white pages · narrative · typewriter and highlighter

D · The Storyboard Open →

A Swiss poster on a strict grid: huge wide type, numbered panels that render from pencil to frame one after another, the studio's numbers set huge, one signal orange.

Light · bold · graphic · black and orange
